Nature, City, Memory

The three worlds

that SENTI captures

SENTI captures the quiet traces left behind by nature, city, and memory.

Nature is the scent of rain, wind, forest, and the first light of morning.

City is the stillness of dawn, the cold air, and the emotions hidden betwe

en passing streets. Memory is the warmth of an old room, the tension of a

first day, and the feelings that linger after time has passed. Each collectio

n begins from a different world, but they all return to one idea.

A moment does not disappear. It remains as a scent.
