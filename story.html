STORY OF THE SENTI

SENTI is a premium niche perfume brand that captures the scent of fleeting moments. Born from three words Scent, Sentiment, and French word Sentir, meaning to feel and to smell SENTI carries essence of all three within a single name.

From the stillness of nature to the quiet corners of the city, from emotions carved into the body to memories you thought had faded SENTI records every forgotten moment as a scent, so that the feeling of that fleeting instant stays with you.
